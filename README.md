# Monetary Policy Transmission and Directed Credit in Brazil

This repository contains the initial scaffold for a macroeconometrics research project.

## Research question
Do monetary policy shocks pass through differently to free credit and directed/earmarked credit in Brazil? Does transmission differ when the directed-credit share is high?

## Data source
The primary data source is the Banco Central do Brasil (BCB) SGS API, which provides macroeconomic and financial time series.

## Free vs directed/earmarked credit
- **Free credit**: lending whose rates and allocation are mostly market-determined.
- **Directed/earmarked credit**: lending linked to policy mandates, earmarked programs, or subsidized channels.

## Planned empirical strategy
We will estimate impulse responses using **local projections (LPs)** to quantify credit responses to monetary policy shocks.

## Planned extension
We will estimate **state-dependent local projections** using a high directed-credit-share dummy to test whether transmission changes across regimes.

## Data quality note
All SGS series codes must be **manually verified** before final estimation.
